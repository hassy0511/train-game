# Visual review

Use identical camera, scale, lighting, and ground plane at every gate.

## Canonical views

- FRONT: orthographic when practical
- RIGHT: orthographic and mandatory
- BACK: orthographic when practical
- 3/4: character identity and volume

## Review order

1. outer silhouette
2. head/body and large proportions
3. front-to-back depth
4. limb and major accessory placement
5. face
6. secondary forms
7. color and material

## Critical failures

Do not advance when any of these remains:

- the outer contour clearly differs from the reference;
- an eye, tooth, mouth, or other facial part protrudes unintentionally;
- head, chest, abdomen, hip, or animal back/belly flow fails from the side;
- a major part floats, intersects visibly, or lacks intended thickness;
- feet float or sink;
- a symmetric design has a large accidental mismatch;
- front and side views cannot both match.
